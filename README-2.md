# College Workflow Investigation and Curriculum Audit

This repository contains two related academic deliverables: a proposed redesign of a student administrative workflow and a curriculum audit with a practical, industry-aligned improvement proposal.

## Repository contents

### Task 1 — Workflow investigation

- `Task-1-Workflow/Workflow_Analysis_Report.docx` — current-state analysis, proposed workflow, ownership/statuses, exception handling, test cases, and time-saving analysis.  
- `Task-1-Workflow/evidence/request_dataset.csv` — request-level data used in the quantitative example.  
- `Task-1-Workflow/evidence/TEST_RESULTS.md` — five scenario tests and expected outcomes.  
- `Task-1-Workflow/evidence/CALCULATIONS.md` — time-saving calculation.

### Task 2 — Curriculum audit

- `Task-2-Curriculum/Curriculum_Audit_Report.docx` — curriculum review, KEEP/UPDATE/REDUCE/ADD recommendations, job-description comparison, AI-assisted exercises, credit-change proposal, and capstone concept.  
- `Task-2-Curriculum/Credit_Change_Calculator.xlsx` — editable credit assumptions and formulas.  
- `Task-2-Curriculum/evidence/AI_Exercise_Verification_Log.txt` — AI-use and verification record for five subject exercises.  
- `Task-2-Curriculum/evidence/SOURCES.md` — source URLs and access dates.  
- `Task-2-Curriculum/evidence/CREDIT_CALCULATIONS.md` — credit-change arithmetic.  
- `Task-2-Curriculum/evidence/AI_USE_AND_CONTRIBUTION.md` — AI-use record and summary of student contribution.  
- `Task-2-Curriculum/evidence/LIMITATIONS.md` — evidence boundaries and limitations.

## Key calculations

- **Workflow:** baseline effort 635 min; proposed effort 440 min; net saving 195 min (3 h 15 min), or approximately 30.7%.  
- **Curriculum proposal:** 36 credits reviewed out of a modelled 160-credit baseline (22.5%); 6 credits reduced and 6 credits added/updated; net credit change 0\.

Supporting calculations and assumptions are detailed in each task folder. See the limitations note before interpreting planning figures as measured institutional facts.

## Sources

Source URLs and the access date (24 October 2026\) are listed in `Task-2-Curriculum/evidence/SOURCES.md`. The official regulation for the exact 2023–2027 batch was not provided, so the public programme-structure page is a reference rather than confirmation of the batch-specific curriculum.

## Test results

The Task 1 folder contains five workflow test scenarios: normal request, missing information, duplicate, unauthorized request, and failed step. These are expected outcomes for a proposed design, not execution logs from a deployed system.

## AI use and personal contribution

The AI-use record is in `Task-2-Curriculum/evidence/AI_USE_AND_CONTRIBUTION.md` and the detailed exercise log is included under `Task-2-Curriculum/evidence/`. The student supplied requirements and course context, identified priorities, and reviewed the deliverables; AI assistance supported drafting, analysis organization, and artifact preparation.

## Limitations

- Generated example request records are used for the workflow analysis; they are not institutional system records.  
- The 160-credit curriculum baseline is a proposed planning model, not a verified official programme total.  
- The exact batch-specific curriculum regulation was not independently confirmed.  
- Test cases are acceptance expectations, not proof of a deployed implementation.  
- Graduate feedback was limited to one respondent; faculty feedback was unavailable.  
- Public job descriptions may change or expire.
