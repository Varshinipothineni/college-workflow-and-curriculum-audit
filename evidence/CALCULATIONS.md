# Workflow Time-Saving Calculation

The report uses the following baseline and proposed-workflow figures:

- Baseline active effort: 635 minutes
- Proposed routine handling: 350 minutes (50 requests × 7 minutes per request)
- Proposed review/support effort: 90 minutes
- Proposed total: 350 + 90 = 440 minutes
- Net saving: 635 − 440 = 195 minutes (3 hours 15 minutes)
- Percentage reduction: 195 ÷ 635 × 100 ≈ 30.7%

The calculation is arithmetically consistent. Its validity as an estimate depends on the stated assumptions and should be confirmed with observed process timings before being treated as a measured institutional result.
