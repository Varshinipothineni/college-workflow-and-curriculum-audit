# Evidence and Limitations

- The exact official curriculum regulation for the 2023–2027 batch was not supplied. The public IFHE programme-structure page and the student-provided Semester VII timetable are distinguished in the report.
- The proposed 160-credit baseline is a planning model, not a verified official programme total.
- Workflow request rows are generated example records for analysis; they are not records extracted from ICFAI/IFHE systems.
- Workflow test cases specify expected behavior; they were not executed against a deployed workflow application.
- AI-assisted exercises document prompts and verification steps. They do not claim that all checks were executed against live production systems.
- Graduate feedback represents one respondent; faculty feedback was not obtained.
- Job descriptions are snapshots accessed on 24 October 2026 and may change.
