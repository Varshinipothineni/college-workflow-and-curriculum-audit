# Proposed Credit-Change Calculation

The report models a 160-credit baseline and reviews 36 credits:

- Review scope: 36 ÷ 160 × 100 = 22.5%
- Proposed reduction: 6 credits
- Proposed addition/update: 6 credits
- Net change: −6 + 6 = 0 credits
- Modelled revised total: 160 credits

These are planning assumptions used to demonstrate a balanced redesign. Verify the baseline and course-credit rules against the official regulation for the relevant programme and admission batch before treating them as official curriculum totals.
